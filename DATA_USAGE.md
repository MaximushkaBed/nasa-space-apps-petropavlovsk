## Use of Generative AI

Our team used OpenAI Codex for research and software development. Codex helped us review scientific papers and public documentation, compare fire-monitoring tools, and explore methods for harmonizing MODIS and VIIRS observations. We also used it to write, debug, and test code for the data pipeline, fire activity calendar, and interactive atlas, and to draft technical documentation.

Our team made the project decisions and checked key findings against published sources, satellite observations, and test results. The deployed application calculates its results from NASA FIRMS observations and other documented datasets using statistical and geospatial code.
